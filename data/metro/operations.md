# Ahmedabad Metro - operations (snapshot 2026-10-10)

Operator: Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation Limited (GMRC), HQ Block No.1, Karmayogi Bhavan, Sector 10/A, Gandhinagar.

## Verified (with source)
- Official GMRC route sheet, https://www.gujaratmetrorail.com/ahmedabad/wp-content/uploads/RoutInfo/Route.pdf (undated; the Phase 2 section is marked "WORK IN PROGRESS"):
  - Blue (East-West), weekdays peak 08:00-11:00 and 17:00-20:00: every 9 min. Weekdays off-peak and weekends: every 10 min (weekend wording on the sheet is 12 min for the NS line; treat as 10-12 min).
  - Blue first train 06:20 from Vastral Gam / Thaltej Gam; last train 22:00 / 22:05.
  - Blue run time: Vastral Gam to Thaltej Gam 45 min 19 s; reverse 43 min 28 s. About 9 min per station hop on the sheet.
  - Red (North-South, APMC to Motera Stadium): every 12 min (sheet also lists 10 min per hop). First train 06:20; last 22:09 from APMC, 22:05 from Motera. Run time about 32 min.
  - Yellow Phase 2 (Motera Stadium to Mahatma Mandir): peak every 30-70 min per sheet (WORK IN PROGRESS). Run time about 46 min end to end.
  - Violet GNLU to GIFT City: about 8 min run time; first train 07:43 from GNLU, last 18:13.
- Fares: GMRC Fare Rules page, https://www.gujaratmetrorail.com/ahmedabad/fare-rules/ (scraped 2026-10-10). Fare products: single, return, group (10+). Fare media: QR paper/mobile, token/CSC, NCMC (10% discount). Token/CSC is valid only inside Phase 1 or inside Phase 2; no travel across the phases. Mobile QR valid 180 min, paper QR 30 min. Fare slabs themselves are NOT on that page (dynamic pricing via the GMRC app).
- Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmedabad_Metro (snapshot 2026): 54 stations, 67.56 km, 3-coach Hyundai Rotem trains, average speed 33 km/h, top speed 80 km/h, annual ridership 5.1 crore (2025-26), opened 4 Mar 2019.
- Instagram reel (undated, not authoritative): fare Rs 5 minimum, Rs 25 maximum; hours 09:00-20:00 quoted for an old schedule. Do not cite as fact.

## ASSUMPTIONS for demo use (not verified)
- Operating hours: 06:00-22:30 daily (route sheet shows 06:20 first and about 22:00 last).
- Fare slabs: Rs 10 (1-2 stations), Rs 15-25 mid-range, max about Rs 30 end-to-end. Real fare is dynamic; check the GMRC app.
- Coach capacity: 3 coaches per train, about 300 passengers per coach (ASSUMPTION; seated plus standing).
- Average inter-station time: about 2-3 min on Phase 1 (route sheet shows 9-10 min per hop, which looks like averaged headway, so do not rely on it for hop times).
- Daily ridership: about 1.4 lakh/day, derived from 5.1 crore/year (ASSUMPTION).
- Navratri late-night operation (Indian Express, https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/ahmedabad/ahmedabad-metro-to-operate-till-2-am-during-navratri-10269139/): extended until 02:00 during Navratri (seen in search excerpt only, not fully read).
